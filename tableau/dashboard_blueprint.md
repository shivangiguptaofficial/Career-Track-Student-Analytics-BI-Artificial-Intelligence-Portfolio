# Tableau Dashboard Design Specification: Student Success & Career Tracks

## Dashboard Layout & Structure
* **Theme:** Professional Dark/Light Blue Corporate UI.
* **Target Audience:** Academy Directors, Curriculum Managers, Student Success Leads.

### KPI Summary Cards (Top Row)
1. **Total Students Enrolled:** Unique student count across all tracks.
2. **Overall Completion Rate:** Percentage of completed enrollments vs total enrollments.
3. **Average Completion Duration:** Mean number of days required to finish a track.

### Visualizations
* **Chart 1: Enrollment Volume by Track (Bar Chart)**
  * *Dimensions:* Track Name
  * *Measures:* Count of Student IDs
  * *Purpose:* Identifies the most and least popular tracks.
* **Chart 2: Enrollment vs. Completion Funnel (Dual Axis / Stacked Bar)**
  * *Dimensions:* Track Name
  * *Measures:* Enrolled vs Completed Counts
  * *Purpose:* Highlights drop-off rates and completion bottlenecks per track.
* **Chart 3: Monthly Enrollment Trend (Line Graph)**
  * *Dimensions:* Enrollment Month (Continuous Date)
  * *Measures:* Count of Enrollments
  * *Purpose:* Tracks acquisition velocity and seasonal interest over time.
